<div data-lang-panel="en">
  <p>As a Cloud Support Engineer specialized in OCI Networking, I provide advanced technical support for enterprise customers using Oracle Cloud Infrastructure networking services. The role combines deep troubleshooting, cloud networking fundamentals, and clear customer communication during incidents that involve connectivity, performance, configuration, and availability.</p>

  <p>My work focuses on investigating and resolving technical cases related to VCNs, subnets, route tables, security lists, NSGs, gateways, load balancers, DNS, NAT Gateway, Service Gateway, DRG, LPG, FastConnect, and IPSec VPN. I analyze hybrid connectivity between on-premises environments and OCI, reviewing routing, firewalling, DNS, private access, peering, and traffic flow to isolate where an issue is happening.</p>

  <p>The position requires strong networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP, routing, subnetting, NAT, DNS, VPN, BGP, TLS/SSL, firewalls, load balancing, and high availability. I also work with Linux networking tools such as ping, traceroute, dig, nslookup, curl, tcpdump, netstat, and ip route, along with logs, metrics, packet captures, and internal diagnostic tools.</p>

  <p>Beyond the technical analysis, a key part of the role is communication and ownership: explaining findings, mitigations, and next steps to customers; documenting solutions, procedures, and RCA details; and collaborating with engineering, operations, and product teams when complex issues or platform bugs need escalation.</p>

  <p>This role connects my background in control and automation engineering with cloud infrastructure at enterprise scale: understanding systems, following signals, isolating dependencies, and keeping a calm, analytical approach during critical incidents.</p>
</div>
