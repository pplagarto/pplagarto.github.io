As a Service Engineer at Ingenieria Operativa, I performed preventive and corrective maintenance for security inspection equipment used in critical environments across Mexico. My work included X-ray machines, signal blockers, walk-through metal detector arches, narcotics and explosives detection systems, and related airport security infrastructure.

I worked with equipment from brands such as Astrophysics and EIYA, among others, supporting systems used for baggage, cargo, access control, and security screening operations. Based on the type of equipment handled by Provetecnia and similar providers in this field, the environment involved technologies such as X-ray inspection for small and large packages, cargo, pallets, documented baggage, mobile inspection, and people-screening technologies such as metal detection and millimeter-wave inspection.

One of my main responsibilities was supporting the security inspection equipment at Mexico City International Airport, Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. For at least one year, I was responsible for maintaining approximately 45 machines in operation, coordinating preventive service, corrective interventions, technical diagnosis, parts follow-up, and field response.

During this period, I helped reach approximately 98% equipment operability. This was important not only from a technical perspective, but also contractually: keeping the equipment available helped avoid penalties and supported compliance with the service agreement.

A key part of the role was customer relationship management. I built a close support relationship with ANAM, keeping communication clear, direct, and reliable. Over time, the client looked directly for my support because they trusted the follow-up, the urgency management, and the technical ownership I brought to each incident.

This experience strengthened my field engineering discipline: diagnosing hardware and system failures under pressure, documenting service activities, coordinating logistics across different locations, and understanding how technical reliability directly affects customer satisfaction and contract performance.
