At ATAV, I worked as a programmer and field engineer for smart home and automation projects. My work was focused on implementation rather than commercial proposal: I configured, programmed, tested, and supported integrated systems directly in the field.

The projects involved Control4 as one of the main automation platforms, along with complementary brands and systems such as Dahua, Lutron, lighting control, audio, cameras, shades, and other connected devices. I programmed scenes, user interfaces, device integrations, and project-specific control logic so customers could manage their spaces in a practical and reliable way.

As a field engineer, I also handled troubleshooting and commissioning on site. This required understanding how each device behaved physically and how it interacted with the rest of the system: network connectivity, controller communication, programmed scenes, customer expectations, and final usability.

I created manuals and user guidance for customers, translating technical system names and project-specific logic into instructions that were easy to follow. These documents became useful when clients needed changes, fixes, or onboarding for new users, and helped maintain a clearer support relationship after delivery.

This experience gave me a strong foundation in technical ownership, customer-facing troubleshooting, and integrated systems thinking. It also became the practical base for my later role at Intelite, where I moved closer to proposal engineering, budgets, post-sales support, and solution design.
